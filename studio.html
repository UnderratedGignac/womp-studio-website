Fish Aquarium — flagship, in development

Three worlds, one save

A touch-only, landscape-only hybrid: part aquarium life-sim, part wave defense, part idle tycoon. Three linked play surfaces sharing one save.

01

Defense Run

Guard the reef

Endless waves around a Guardian Tower. Start with $500 and your deck; a boss every fifth wave. 64 species, each with one timed skill — damage, stun, venom, nova, slow, shield, feed, gold.

02

Pleco Rampage

One pleco, many guns

The fighter layer inside the run: a gun-carrying, skin-collecting pleco climbing an ascension wall. Feed-and-grow underneath — elders pay back 1.8×, every meal compounds.

03

Aquarium Sanctuary

An idle estate

A full-screen, borderless aquarium business: grow the collection, tend the estate, earn while the tank runs itself. One save links all three surfaces.

In active development — Android build in testing. Monetized with server-verified opt-in rewarded ads and a Supabase backend for cloud saves, leaderboards and referrals.

How it's built

Serious stack, small team

Engine

Hand-rolled HTML5 canvas + Pixi.js UI. Object pooling, sprite atlases, asset pre-warming — built to hold 60 FPS on mid-range Android GPUs.

Android

Capacitor 8, landscape-locked. Edge-to-edge rendering with safe-area and cutout handling; AAB releases through Gradle.

Backend

Supabase. Cloud saves, leaderboards, referral graph — serverless, scales from zero.

QA

500+ headless Vitest suites, plus touch-target and layout regression gates for phone viewports from 568×320 up.

Monetization

Unity Ads, server-verified rewards. Every rewarded payout validated server-side — opt-in only, no pay-to-win.

Toolchain

Vite builds, generative art pipeline. Sprite generation, packing and optimization scripted end to end; CI on every push.
